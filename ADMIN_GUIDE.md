# Admin Guide: Managing Materials

This guide explains how to add, edit, and organize the items in the **Recently Added Material** section of the Roger Williams Exhibit.

---

### 1. Logging In
To access the management tools:
1.  Navigate to the **Recently Added Material** page on the website.
2.  Scroll to the very bottom of the page (the footer).
3.  Click the small **Admin** link next to the copyright text.
4.  Enter your credentials:
    -   **Email:** `ally.jacobs@vanderbilt.edu`
    -   **Password:** `allyjacobs`

---

### 2. Adding New Materials
Once logged in, a "Upload New Material" section will appear at the top:
1.  **Title:** Enter a name for the collection (e.g., "Class Portraits").
2.  **Select Files:** Click "Choose Files" to pick one or more images or PDFs.
3.  **Descriptions:** For every file you select, a small box will appear. Enter a description for that specific item.
4.  **Upload:** Click the "Upload" button to save it to the exhibit.

---

### 3. Organizing & Editing
You can manage existing materials directly on the page:

*   **Change Order:** Click and hold the **dotted handle (⠿)** in the top-right of any card to drag it into a new position. The order saves automatically.
*   **Edit Details:** Click the **Pencil Icon** to change the Title or update the descriptions for individual slides in a gallery.
*   **Delete:** Click the **Red X** to permanently remove a material from the gallery.

---

### 4. Grouping Multiple Files
If you upload multiple photos at once, they will automatically be grouped into a **slideshow (carousel)**. 
- Visitors can click the arrows to flip through them.
- The description text at the bottom will automatically change to match whichever photo is currently being viewed.

---

### 5. Logging Out
When you are finished, click the **Logout** link in the footer. This will hide the admin tools and allow you to see the gallery exactly as a regular visitor would.
